Health awareness

Know when to consult a physician

Early medical advice can help clarify symptoms and guide appropriate care.

01

Persistent symptoms

Seek medical advice when fever, cough, digestive discomfort or fatigue persists or worsens.

02

Changes in readings

Unusual blood sugar or blood pressure readings deserve timely professional evaluation.

03

Ongoing condition review

Regular follow-up helps review symptoms, medicines, test results and lifestyle guidance.

This information is for general educational purposes and does not replace consultation with a qualified medical professional. In an emergency, contact local emergency services immediately.
